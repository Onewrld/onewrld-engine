## What it means
1. **TikTok already sends real traffic** (191 visits in 90 days, 30% of all visits), so content works at getting people to the store.
2. **The store loses them at checkout.** About 19 people reached checkout and 1 finished. This is the biggest money leak in the business, and it's upstream of content volume: more traffic into a broken funnel mostly makes more abandoned checkouts.
3. **Most sales happen in person** (events, gyms). Online sales are near zero, so "content to online sales" is still unproven.

## Abandoned checkouts (recent, cleaned)
- The two $1,116 checkouts on Nov 2 contained the **entire hoodie stock** (every size, every unit). Almost certainly the owner or a test, not customers. Excluded.
- Real-looking ones: Fall of Icarus hoodie and sweats ($47 to $177 carts), the hat ($22 to $23), the shaker cup ($8). Two used affiliate/rep discount codes, so the rep program is driving buyers to checkout.
- **Shipping is added at checkout:** a $42.50 cart became $52.49 (+$9.99), a $46.95 cart became $53.94 (+$6.99). A surprise $7 to $10 on a $47 item is a classic abandonment cause.
- Most abandoned before entering an address, meaning they left at the very first checkout screen.
- Unclear: the hat shows $22.68 to $23.42 at checkout vs. $21.99 listed. Not sure why; worth checking the hat's price and variants.

## Fixes (in Shopify admin, no code)
1. **Shipping:** free shipping on Fall of Icarus pieces (fold about $5 into the price) or a free-shipping threshold, and **say so on the product page**.
2. **Turn on abandoned-checkout emails** (Shopify Marketing automations, free). Every abandoned checkout above that would have gotten a reminder.
3. **Check payment options:** Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal on. Fast wallets lift mobile checkout, and TikTok traffic is almost all mobile.
4. **Do one real test purchase on a phone,** from TikTok's in-app browser, to the end. Watch for anything confusing (the pre-order app is active on the theme; make sure it isn't changing in-stock items).
5. Re-run this audit 2 weeks after the fixes.

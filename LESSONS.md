# LESSONS.md

Durable rules learned from ONEWRLD's own data. The content generator reads this file before it creates anything.

Format: rule · evidence · confidence · date.
Two kinds of evidence: **owner preference** (your reviews and quiz) and **performance** (real post and sales data, from Phase 6 on). Performance lessons need minimum sample sizes; owner-preference lessons are the owner's word and apply right away.

## Owner preference

- **L-001: Don't reinterpret Fall of Icarus as "fly anyway" or "the fall is a badge of honor."** The owner rejected all 6 captions built on that angle ("not the story", "takes away the point of the story"). **Meaning confirmed by the owner (2026-11-02): pride comes before the fall.** Icarus got arrogant, ignored the warning, flew too high and fell. Discipline and ego in check. Stored in `brand/brand_bible.toml` → `[collections.fall_of_icarus]`. · evidence: 6/6 rejected, 2026-11-02 · confidence: high
- **L-002: What gets approved: plain, statement-led, slightly exclusive, real product facts.** Approved: "Nobody gave us a head start. We stopped waiting for one." / "They'll call it luck once it works. Let them." / "Not for everyone. That's the point." / "480 GSM cotton. You feel the weight the second you pick it up." · evidence: 4/4 approved, 2026-11-02 · confidence: medium (small sample)
- **L-003: Voice quiz profile (2026-11-02):** product over story · longer lines OK · "I" over "we", but brand voice over founder voice · no call to action · statements, not questions · plain language over myth references · no emoji · hashtags OK · no scarcity talk, but sold-out proof is OK · no Staten Island mention · gym implied, not explicit · sentence case · exclusive over inclusive. · confidence: medium (one quiz pass; some answers pull in different directions, e.g. "I" vs brand voice)
- **L-004: Product facts from the owner's own product pages (safe to use):** hoodie: 480 GSM 100% cotton, ONEWRLD emblem embroidered on the chest, Fall of Icarus motif printed DTG on the back, unconventional stitching, "restraint and boldness". Sweatpants: heavy cotton, wash darker in the folds and lighter where it catches, wide and relaxed from the knee down, stacks over sneakers, DTG graphics. · confidence: high
- **L-005: Exclusive, never rude.** "Not for everyone. That's the point." was approved, but "If it needs explaining, it isn't for you." and "Not made for the loud ones." were rejected as rude. Rule: describe who it IS for. Never talk down to who it isn't for. · evidence: 1 approved, 2 rejected ("its rude"), 2026-11-02 · confidence: high
- **L-006: No bragging. Sold-out flexes read as cocky.** "Every tee and collab we've made sold out…" was rejected as "cocky", even though the quiz leaned toward sold-out proof. When a review and the quiz disagree, **the review wins** (it's a real decision about a real caption). · evidence: 1 rejected, 2026-11-02 · confidence: medium
- **L-007: Product captions must be concrete and physical, not abstract or poetic.** Approved: weight, stitching, fit ("Wide from the knee down. Stacks over your sneakers without looking sloppy."), what's on the chest and back. Rejected: abstract pairings ("Restraint and boldness. That's the whole hoodie.") and fancy wording that doesn't read clearly ("lighter where they catch the light", "doesnt make sense"). · evidence: 4 approved, 2 rejected, 2026-11-02 · confidence: medium
- **L-008: Approval rate so far:** batch 1: 4/10 (40%). Batch 2: 5/10 (50%). Product captions: 5/7 approved (71%), the strongest category. Lean on product. · 2026-11-02
